Frequently asked questions

What license does a California pool builder need?

The Contractors State License Board's C-53 Swimming Pool license covers building and remodeling pools, including plastering. A B General Building contractor may take on a pool as part of a larger project. Every business in this directory holds a C-53 license, checked against CSLB records.

Does a new or remodeled pool need a permit and safety features?

Yes, a building permit, and in California a permit to build or remodel a pool or spa at a single-family home requires at least two of the seven drowning-prevention safety features listed in Health and Safety Code section 115922. Ask the builder which two are in the price.

How much can a pool builder ask for as a down payment?

On a California home improvement contract, no more than $1,000 or 10 percent of the contract price, whichever is less. Progress payments should not run ahead of the work actually done.

What does "suspended" mean on a listing?

CSLB has suspended the license, usually because a bond was cancelled, insurance lapsed or a judgment went unpaid. A suspended contractor may not contract for or perform work until CSLB reinstates the license. Check the license live on the day you sign.

What does "workers' comp exempt" mean?

The contractor has certified to CSLB that it has no employees. If a crew shows up to build your pool, ask whose employees they are and for proof of workers' compensation coverage.

Is the $25,000 contractor's bond enough protection?

Rarely. It is the legal minimum, and it is shared by everyone with a claim against that license. On a pool that costs ten times that, the contract is your real protection.
