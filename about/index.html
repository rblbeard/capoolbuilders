About the publisher

CA Pool Builders is published by Randy Beard, a licensed California pool contractor in Costa Mesa.

Randy has worked in the pool trade since 1979. He built and ran a pool construction company of more than 60 employees, is a former investigator for the Contractors State License Board, and holds the C-53 Swimming Pool and C-27 Landscaping licenses (CSLB #871188, C-53 Swimming Pool and C-27 Landscaping). He has given deposition testimony in more than 100 construction defect matters and trial testimony in San Diego and Los Angeles Superior Courts, for plaintiffs and defendants.

How this directory is built

  • Every licensed C-53 pool contractor in the CSLB public files is included, except Randy's own license and any builder connected to a matter Randy is working on.
  • Facts come only from the state's records, with the date of the data on every page. Nothing is rated or ranked.
  • Builders are grouped by the city on their CSLB mailing address. Street addresses are not shown.
  • No builder pays to be listed, and Randy receives nothing for any referral.
