# Pool contract review before you sign

**Picked a builder and have a bid?** This is the step before you sign.

You send the contract and plans. Randy Beard reads them the way he would if the job had already gone wrong and he had been hired as the expert, and tells you what to fix before you sign.

## What you get

  * A check of the contract against California's home improvement and pool contract requirements
  * What the plans show and what they leave to the builder's discretion (shell thickness, steel, drainage, equipment)
  * The questions to ask the builder, and the changes to request, in writing
  * A license check on the builder and the people on the license



## Cost: $495 flat per bid

One price for one residential pool bid: the contract, the plans and the specifications the builder gave you. It includes a written summary of what to fix and what to ask, and a 30-minute call to go over it. Comparing a second builder's bid for the same pool is $250 more. Commercial pools, HOA pools and projects needing a site visit are quoted before any work starts. You pay before the review begins, and you get it back in full if Randy finds a conflict with your builder.

## How to start

Email the contract, plans and the builder's name to [rb@varx1.com](mailto:rb@varx1.com), or call (949) 264-3466. Before any review starts, Randy checks that he has no conflict with the builder.

Randy Beard receives nothing from any builder, and no builder pays to appear in this directory.
