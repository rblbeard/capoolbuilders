Corrections

Listings repeat the Contractors State License Board's public records as of the date shown. If your license record has changed, the listing updates with the next data refresh. If something on a page does not match your CSLB record, email rb@varx1.com with your license number and it will be checked against CSLB and fixed.

To change the underlying record itself (status, bond, personnel), contact CSLB.
