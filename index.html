Why this exists

Randy Beard has worked in the California pool trade since 1979, is a former CSLB investigator, and has given deposition testimony in more than 100 construction defect matters. Most of the pool disputes he sees started with a contract the owner signed without reading closely. A license check takes two minutes. A contract review before signing costs far less than a lawsuit after. More about Randy.
