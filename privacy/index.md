# Privacy

This site does not use advertising trackers. If you email us, your message is used only to answer you.

To show pool builders near you, the home page asks the site's host (Cloudflare) for your approximate city, which it estimates from your internet address. That happens in your browser to pick which cities to show; we do not store it, log it or use it for anything else.

The chat assistant in the corner is an AI, not Randy. Before it starts, it asks you to agree that the conversation is processed and recorded by our service provider, ElevenLabs, so it can answer and so Randy can follow up if you ask him to. Chat records are kept for 90 days. If you ask the assistant to pass your details to Randy, it sends your name, contact details, city and question to him and to no one else. Please don't share payment details or other sensitive information in the chat.
