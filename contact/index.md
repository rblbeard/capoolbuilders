## What to contact Randy about

  * **A bid you haven't signed yet.** Randy reviews one residential pool bid (contract, plans and specifications) for a flat $495. [How contract review works](https://capoolbuilders.com/contract-review/).

## What to expect

Randy usually replies within one business day. Before any paid review he checks for a conflict with the builder; if there is one, he says so and any payment is refunded in full. Builders do not pay to be listed, nothing on this site is ranked, and Randy receives nothing for referrals. Randy cannot give legal advice; for legal questions, talk to a California attorney.

You can also ask the site assistant (the chat button at the bottom of the page) a general question and have it pass your details to Randy.
