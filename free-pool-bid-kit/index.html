Free California Pool Bid Kit

Bids that look cheaper often just leave things out. This free 7-page PDF shows what a complete pool bid should list, so you can compare builders on the same terms before you sign.

Download the free PDF (7 pages, no sign-up)

What's inside

  • Part 1: What a complete pool bid lists. About 70 line items in 12 groups: design and permits, excavation and haul-off, steel and shell, plumbing, equipment, electrical and gas, tile and finish, decking, safety, extras, handover, and allowances and exclusions. Each line has a "Yes / No" box and a blank for the builder's price. Hand it to each builder and ask them to fill it in.
  • Part 2: Bid comparison sheet. Put three bids side by side: price, down payment, equipment models, finish, allowances, safety features, schedule and warranty.
  • Part 3: 25 questions to ask a pool builder, from license and insurance to rock clauses, lien releases and warranties.
  • Part 4: Contract must-haves under California law (Business and Professions Code 7159, 7159.5 and 7167).

Written by Randy Beard, licensed California pool contractor (CSLB #871188, C-53 Swimming Pool and C-27 Landscaping), in the pool trade since 1979, former CSLB investigator and construction defect expert witness. There are no prices or price ranges in the kit: every yard is different, and the point is to get every builder to price the same complete job.

General information, not legal advice, and not a substitute for engineering or permit plans.
